# MT7601U

## Captured Wireless Card

- MediaTek MT7601U, 1x1 2.4 GHz 802.11b/g/n, one TX chain and one RX chain.
- Tested device: generic `148f:7601` dongle, USB 2.0 high-speed bulk endpoints.
- Captures: `driver_captures_mt7601u/`, gitignored; reproduce them with the scripts below.

## Linux Driver Source

- Mainline Linux `drivers/net/wireless/mediatek/mt7601u/` at tag `v7.2`, commit `8d3ae59288f1e7d58d76558a6ee96d533bc5019f`, 2026-08-16.
- Source URL: `https://github.com/torvalds/linux/tree/v7.2/drivers/net/wireless/mediatek/mt7601u`.
- Vendored reference path: `driver_sources/mt7601u-source-v7.2/mt7601u/`, gitignored.

## Python Port Details

- Claims the 17 VID:PIDs in the kernel's `mt7601u_device_table` through `SUPPORTED_IDS` and lazy `import_driver()`.
- `148f:760a` overlaps MT76x0; `device.manager._resolve_148f_760a` selects the package from `MT_ASIC_VERSION`.
- Port status: Receive+Inject-Monitor; TX remains user-gated.
- Replay gates cover EEPROM, firmware, station-memory clears and 55 channel tunes.
- `verify_tx.py` accepts a byte match against either of two descriptor variants; it does not prove over-air emission.
- No code is shared with another chip driver.

**Non-obvious in the port**

- RX bulk reads must start after `mcu_cmd_init()` and before `write_mac_initvals()`; other positions produced no RX data on the tested card.
- `RX_FILTER_MONITOR` is `PROMISC|DUP` (`0x14`); broader filter values suppressed unicast in tests on the captured card.
- `MAX_ACK_DELAY` is 0.25 seconds; measured ACK arrival was 2.1–119.8 ms on two dongles and exceeded the base 20 ms window in 14 of 15 trials.
- Firmware lookup checks system firmware paths first and the packaged `assets/mt7601u.bin` fallback last.
- A kernel-bound device is refused before PyUSB detaches it because a kernel-initialized warm device produced no RX data in userspace tests.

## Known Problems

- TX requires the user hardware gate; successful `bulk_out` and descriptor replay do not establish RF emission.
- Association, pairwise keys and AP mode are not ported; PMKID, WPS and EvilTwin campaigns are unavailable.
- WCID allocation and programming helpers exist but are not connected to an association path.
- Warm reattach is unsupported; unplug and replug after the kernel driver has initialized the device.
- The port exposes channels 1–14 and does not apply an nl80211 regulatory-domain check.

## Driver Entry Points

- Bring-up: `driver.py:MT7601UDriver.connect` and `init.py:MT7601UInit`.
- Channel tuning: `phy.py:MT7601UPhy.set_channel`.
- RX: `rx.py:iter_frames`; TX: `tx.py:build_tx_dma` and `tx_ring.py:TxQueue.submit`.
- Firmware: `firmware.py:find_firmware` and `firmware.py:load_firmware`.
- USB transport: `transport.py:MT7601UTransport`.

## Scripts

- `verify_mt7601u.py`: replay EEPROM, firmware and station-memory stages from a capture.
- `verify_tune.py`: replay and compare recorded channel tunes.
- `verify_tx.py`: compare recorded TX info/TXWI headers; capture truncation excludes the frame body.
- `gen_constants.py` and `gen_initvals.py`: regenerate source-derived constants and tables.
- `capture_mt7601u.py`, `usbmon.py` and `find_tunes.py`: capture helpers; `find_tunes.py` is safe to delete.
